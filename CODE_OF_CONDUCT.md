# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: **be respectful**. Disagreements about technical decisions
are welcome and encouraged; personal attacks, harassment, or
discriminatory language are not.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, email
markus@markusnissl.com. All reports are confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may warn, temporarily ban, or permanently ban any
contributor whose behavior violates this code, at their discretion.
